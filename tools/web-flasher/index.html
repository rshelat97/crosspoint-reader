CrossPoint Flasher

Install this fork's CrossPoint firmware over USB from Chrome or Edge on a computer. Nothing to install. Back to the simulator.

This browser can't talk to USB devices.

Use Chrome, Edge or another Chromium browser on a desktop computer (Web Serial is required).

1. Choose firmware

2. Connect and flash

  1. Connect the device with a USB-C data cable and wake it.
  2. Click Connect and pick the device (often "USB JTAG/serial debug unit").

Device doesn't show up? It may be USB-locked

Some X3/X4 units sold by third-party stores have USB flashing disabled by a one-time fuse burned at the factory. No software can undo that, but the device can still be updated without USB. First try another cable (many are charge-only), another port, and waking the device. The About screen of this fork's firmware shows USB Flashing: Locked on such units.

Device runs the stock Xteink firmware
  1. Use the CrossPoint OTA Unlocker. It runs a Wi-Fi hotspot on your computer and answers the device's own update check with CrossPoint.
  2. Then follow the next section to move to this fork.
Device already runs CrossPoint (this fork or upstream)

Over Wi-Fi: Settings → System → Check for Updates. This fork's builds update from this fork's releases. Upstream builds update from upstream, so use the SD card once to switch.

From the SD card (CrossPoint 1.3.0 or newer):

  1. Download the firmware for your device from step 1 (or the Releases page).
  2. Copy the .bin to the SD card, or upload it with File Transfer.
  3. Settings → System → SD Card Firmware Update, then pick the file.
Nothing else works

Writing the flash chip directly with an external programmer always works, but means opening the device: see Recovering a bricked Xteink.
